## English summary

- **Code licence: AGPL-3.0-only.** The source is free software: you may use, study, modify and redistribute it under the GNU Affero General Public License v3. Like every OSI-approved licence, it **permits commercial use** by others; it requires derivative works distributed or offered over a network to remain under the same licence. The project itself is operated as a non-commercial fan utility.
- **Fan utility, non-commercial operation.** No revenue, no ads, no accounts, no telemetry. Not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Konami Digital Entertainment, Shueisha, TV Tokyo or Studio Dice.
- **Konami deck-list form is not redistributed.** `data/templates/KDE_DeckList.pdf` is downloaded from Konami on first use (`ygo fetch-data`) or supplied with `YGO_KDE_TEMPLATE`; it ships with neither the repository nor the npm package and is used only to fill a blank form.
- **Local-first.** All processing happens on your machine; the only outbound traffic is read-only public APIs (card data, prices, news). Deck data and custom cards never leave your disk.
- **Fair-use basis.** Card names, stats and images are referenced for research, study and personal strategy analysis, without replacing the commercial product.
- **Immediate takedown.** The project will be removed immediately upon any legitimate request from a rights holder or competent authority. Contact: an issue or a private advisory on the repository.
- **Consent to removal.** The maintainer does not contest such a request and will remove the repository and the published npm package without requiring legal action.
- **AI-generated content.** The code, tests and documentation were produced almost entirely by AI agents under human supervision, review and testing. This disclosure follows Vietnam's AI Law 134/2025 and Decree 142/2026, and applies to forks and repackaged versions too.
- **AI answers are not guaranteed to be correct.** Every ruling, deck suggestion, price and explanation is informational and must be verified against the cited source before it is relied on. The authors accept no liability for decisions taken from an answer that was not checked.
- **Beta software, no warranty.** The project is under active development; defects and breaking changes are expected. It is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. Check for updates (`npx -y yugioh-assistant-mcp@latest --version`, the releases page) before relying on it, and pin a version for production use.
- **Rulings are advisory.** Official tournament rulings belong to the event's head judge. Price data is informational; the authors accept no liability for trades or purchases made using it.
- **Third-party data is attributed.** Every data source, licence and usage rule is listed in [`docs/CREDITS.md`](CREDITS.md). No source is mirrored or redistributed; responses are fetched read-only and cached locally. Yugipedia text is CC BY-SA 3.0. Price coverage is EN/JP/AE only — KR and other languages have no public feed and are reported as `null`, never estimated.
